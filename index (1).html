Mabuhay & Welcome

Your Resort Getaway Starts Here

Welcome, traveler! Davao Oriental invites you to slow down, breathe in the sea breeze, and experience a province rich in natural beauty and warm island hospitality. From surf-side villas on Dahican to calm pools overlooking Pujada Bay, every resort on this site was picked to help you relax, explore, and travel responsibly.

Whether you are chasing waves, trekking through ancient forests, or simply looking for a quiet shoreline to call your own for a weekend, here are the resorts waiting to welcome you:

  • Sheepy's Surfside Beach Resort📍 Mati City
  • Dahican Beach Resort and Spa📍 Mati City
  • Pujada Bay Beach Resort📍 Mati City
  • GovGen Beach Resort📍 Governor Generoso
  • Bonhomie Leisure and Resort📍 Banaybanay
  • Costa Celine Beach Resort📍 Cateel

A Few Places Travelers Love

Beyond the resorts, these attractions are worth adding to your itinerary.

  • Dahican Beach — a long stretch of sand loved by surfers and turtle watchers.
  • Mt. Hamiguitan Range Wildlife Sanctuary — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2014, home to a rare pygmy forest.
  • Pujada Bay — a protected seascape perfect for calm-water kayaking and island hopping.
  • Aliwagwag Falls — a breathtaking multi-tiered waterfall in Cateel.
