📍 City of Mati, Davao Oriental

A 7-kilometer stretch of white sand and turquoise water, popular for surfing, skimboarding, and swimming.

About Dahican Beach

Dahican Beach is one of the famous tourist destinations in Mati City, Davao Oriental. It is known for its beautiful white sand, clear blue water, and ocean views.

Activities to Enjoy

  • Surfing and skimboarding
  • Swimming
  • Beach relaxation
  • Watching the sunrise

Things to Remember

  • Keep the beach clean.
  • Follow swimming safety rules.
  • Respect the environment.
