🌴 Why We Made This Site

"Discover Davao Oriental" began as a simple idea: the first place Pacific sunrises touch the Philippines deserves more than a passing mention. We wanted travelers, and our classmates too, to see beyond the headlines and find the everyday beauty of this coastal province — its beaches, forests, bay, and people — all in one easy-to-browse place.

🎯 Our Mission

To promote sustainable, community-friendly tourism by making accurate, accessible information about Davao Oriental's resorts and attractions easy to find, so planning a trip feels as relaxing as the trip itself.

🔭 Our Vision

A Davao Oriental where every visitor leaves with respect for the Mandaya culture and the natural environment, and where these places stay beautiful for generations to come.

🏝️ Why Resorts?

Because a good resort is more than a bed. It is the front-row seat to a surf break, a calm bay, or a quiet cove. We focus on resorts so you can compare where to stay by location, facilities, and what's nearby.

🧳 What Makes It Special

Few provinces offer this much variety so close together. In a single trip you can:

  • Surf at Dahican
  • Kayak Pujada Bay
  • Trek Mt. Hamiguitan
  • Chase Aliwagwag Falls
  • Camp by the sea

🗺️ A Trip Through the Province

  1. Banaybanay – the gateway from Davao City, with beachfront stops along the highway.
  2. Mati City – the capital, home to Dahican Beach, Pujada Bay, and most of our featured resorts.
  3. Governor Generoso – the southern coast, great for camping and quiet shorelines.
  4. Cateel – in the north, where the coast meets waterfalls and rainforest.

🤝 Respect for the Mandaya

The Mandaya are among the indigenous peoples of Davao Oriental, and their culture is woven into the land. When you visit, ask permission before taking photos, listen to local guides, and buy from local vendors. Good travel leaves both people and place better off.
